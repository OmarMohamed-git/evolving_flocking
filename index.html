Hover the canvas to inspect a boid. Click to lock onto one, click again to release.

The coloured arrows are not destinations - each is the difference between where the boid is going and where that rule wants it to go. They shrink to nothing once a flock settles, because there is nothing left to correct. The boid follows neither arrow: it follows their sum.

Try Cohesion alone, with Alignment at 0 - the flock collapses into a dense knot, because nothing keeps the boids apart. That is the problem separation exists to solve.

Predators have no rules yet - they fly straight.
